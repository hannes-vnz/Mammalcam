# Crop classifier vs. full-image baseline

Generated by `python -m mammalcam.models.classify`. Do not edit by hand.

- Both models: frozen `convnext_tiny.fb_in22k` embeddings + logistic regression (balanced class weights, C chosen on the validation cameras)
- Full image: whole image resized to 224×224
- Crops: trained on labelled MegaDetector crops; tested on **every** MegaDetector animal detection (conf ≥ 0.2) of the test image, no ground-truth boxes involved. Crop probabilities are averaged per image, weighted by detection confidence. No detection → counted as wrong.
- Burst average: probabilities averaged over the frames of a burst (sequence).
- Test images: 51,129 images with exactly one of the 12 classes; 11.4% of them have no MegaDetector detection.

## Main result: 5-fold cross-validation over camera groups

Every camera is in the test group exactly once. Pooled = all test predictions together; per fold = mean ± standard deviation over the folds.
