# SpeciesNet vs. own classifier

Generated by `python -m mammalcam.speciesnet.evaluate`. Do not edit by hand.

- Test images: 51,129 (same as for the own models; own predictions from the camera cross-validation, so no model saw the camera of a test image)
- SpeciesNet: v4.0.3a crop classifier on the same MegaDetector boxes, its own ensemble with geofence (country USA, state CA) and taxonomic roll-up; image prediction = most confident detection
- SpeciesNet labels are mapped to the 12 classes by taxonomy (e.g. family Leporidae → rabbit). Anything else counts as wrong.
- Of SpeciesNet's wrong predictions, 1,545 (3.0% of all images) are correct but coarser than the class, e.g. 'canine family' for a coyote.
- Caveat: SpeciesNet's training data is not published per dataset; Caltech Camera Traps is public and may have been part of it.
